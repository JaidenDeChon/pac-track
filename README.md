# pac-track

Who funds whom in US politics, with sources, in neutral terms.

pac-track shows where money reaches elected officials and candidates at the federal and state level: who gave, how much, through which channel, and how that lines up with documented policy positions and votes. Every number links to its filing and every tag links to its evidence.
